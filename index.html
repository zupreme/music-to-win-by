Album statement

A focused electronic release for builders, operators, and people who want a soundtrack layer for software, security, and long work sessions.

The record keeps its language technical and direct. It is meant to sit comfortably beside a runbook, a dashboard, a design review, or a late-night build log without sounding like a marketing exercise.
